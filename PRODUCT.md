# Sam's Worlds

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Sam is seven, learning through an offline-first, touch-friendly family PWA.
His parent explicitly asks for a creative Trait Safari rebuild that teaches the
relationship between traits and adaptations, rather than animal trivia or labels.
Implementation decisions are delegated; no additional interview is requested.

## Constraints
Preserve existing progress, released IDs, parent gates, deterministic rewards,
and fresh-question-after-miss behavior. Use simple English, read-aloud, local
licensed photographs, keyboard access, and at least 48px touch controls.
No accounts, analytics, punishment, runtime image service, or involuntary timers.

## Learning contract
An adaptation is also a trait. Inheritance and useful effects alone do not prove
evolutionary history. Separate known adaptations, traits needing more evidence,
learned skills, acquired changes, and human-made objects. Assess construction
and explanations, not recall of animal names. Evidence and implementation
assumptions are documented in reports/Teaching traits through discovery.md.

## Existing authority
SAMS_GAME_PLAN.md, CLAUDE.md, docs/ANSWER_INTEGRITY.md, the existing navy/amber
touch UI, and the licensed photograph catalog remain the product baseline.
